My eulogy to Dennis Ritchie

In Mexico you have to go through 6 years of elementary school, 3 for secundaria and another three of bachillerato / high-school just before you can get to the University.

During my three years of high-school I was taught the C language. The first paragraphs of my textbook on C talked about Unix and some of the doings of Kernighan, Ritchie and Thompson in the Bell Labs.

Once in the University, one of the first things that I did was to get a hold of The C programming language. I don't remember whether it was in Spanish or English.

Nowadays I work as a Python programmer on Linux systems. And I enjoy it. So thanks Dennis, and farewell.
